# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the `showmrr` server shows a green dot in Customize, then MCPs. Everything here is read-only.

1. `Use ShowMRR to list the top 10 SaaS startups by MRR and tell me which figures are verified.`
2. `Which categories does ShowMRR have? List them.`
3. `Look up <slug> on ShowMRR: current MRR, verification status and the last 90 days of history, with a link to its page.`
4. `Compare <slug-1>, <slug-2> and <slug-3> by MRR and say which numbers are self-reported.`
5. `Show the verified-only leaderboard. If it is empty, say so instead of showing self-reported figures.`
6. `Search ShowMRR for startups about invoicing and show their MRR with verification labels.`
